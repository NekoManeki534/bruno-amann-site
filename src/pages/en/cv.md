## Career

- 1996: Full Professor of Management Science (agrégation)
- 1995: Associate Professor (Maître de conférences), Université Toulouse 1
- 1993: PhD in Management Science, Université Toulouse 1 (“Propriété et maîtrise des risques dans l’entreprise”, supervised by Prof. Alain Couret)
- Initial training in law (postgraduate degrees in business law and criminal law) and management (postgraduate degree in management science, IAE Toulouse)

## Main responsibilities

- Director of LGCO (Governance and Organisational Control Laboratory), 2007-2014
- Dean of IAE des Pays de l’Adour (University of Pau), 2003-2006
- Chair of the Scientific Committee of IUT Toulouse and Vice-Chair of the Research Committee of IUT Midi-Pyrénées, until 2015
- Vice-President of Atlas AFMI (since 2017)

## PhD supervision

Supervision of PhD and DBA theses, mostly co-supervised with J. Jaussaud (University of Pau): see [PhD supervision](/en/phd-supervision/).
